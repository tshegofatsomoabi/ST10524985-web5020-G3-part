About Us

Hope Haven Animal Rescue is a non-profit organisation created to help animals that have been abandoned, neglected or left without proper care.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue vulnerable animals, provide temporary care and help them find responsible and loving homes.

Our Vision

Our vision is a community where animals are treated with kindness and every rescued animal has an opportunity for a better life.

Who We Help

We help abandoned and vulnerable animals while also supporting people who want to adopt, foster or volunteer.
